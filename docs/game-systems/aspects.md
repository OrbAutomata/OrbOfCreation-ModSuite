# Aspects

**World > Aspects** holds the pedestal slots for placed aspects, and it is how the game's major
late systems arrive: Aspect: Workshop, Aspect: Alchemy Lab and Aspect: Rituals each unlock their
system through one of these world slots.

Each of Gather Space, Gather Space II and Gather Space III adds one pedestal (observed: Gather
Space 1e4 Space, Gather Space II 1e7 Space). The Aspects subtab, and with it every aspect upgrade,
stays hidden until the first one is bought, even though Aspect: Workshop and Aspect: Alchemy Lab
have no prerequisites of their own. With a free slot, the aspect upgrades sit in the page's
Upgrades list; with every slot occupied they are refused.

Empty capacity slots elsewhere in the game are real and intentional: the game preserves them in
saves, so an empty row is never evidence of a lost item.
