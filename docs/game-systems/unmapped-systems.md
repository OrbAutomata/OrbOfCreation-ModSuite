# Unmapped systems

Shipped features with confirmed presence in the game that have never been played or written up. Play
them, roughly in the order a run reaches them, and write the page as you go: the data answers "what
exists", only play answers "what it is for".

| System | What is known |
|---|---|
| Rituals | The game's combat layer: a ritual is fought in waves of enemies, and Mysticism's Stability meter is your health while one runs. A research node extends ritual duration. Entry cost, rewards and wave mechanics are unknown. |
| Alchemy | The screen exists with a six-pool loadout, a Materials page, and an Alchemy Level dial whose ceiling is bought separately (see [attributes-and-upgrades.md](attributes-and-upgrades.md)). What alchemy produces, and what the pools are, is unknown. |
| Zeal | Exists — a research node governs recovering it. Nothing else. |
| Dimensional | A World subtab whose attributes feed the Shaper tab. Never opened. |
| Artificer, Construction, Mystic, Shaper | Named disciplines with their own progression tracks and advancement grants. Never played. |
| Artifact effects | The loadout binds weight and slots, and upgrading costs two gear currencies. What artifacts do is unknown. |
| Emblems | 24 emblem passives exist; one is worked out (see [emblems.md](emblems.md)). |
| Plots | Node-level harvest rates and prerequisites underlie Agromancy; only the surface behaviour is known. |
