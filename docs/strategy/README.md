# Strategy

How to play Orb of Creation well: policies, trade-offs, run planning. Mechanics live in
[../game-systems/](../game-systems/README.md) and are never re-explained here.

- [scoring.md](scoring.md) — rate moves −10..+10 against the active goal's clock, in a named
  operating mode.
- [decision-rules.md](decision-rules.md) — the rules that survive an era change: one-way doors,
  watermarks, binding constraints.
- [reservations.md](reservations.md) — good advice is "hold 30 Knowledge for Bandwidth"; floors
  belong to the resource.
- [spend-modes.md](spend-modes.md) — hoard, burst, rest or reinvest, chosen by whichever growth term
  dominates.
- [resource-lifecycle.md](resource-lifecycle.md) — every resource walks scarce to frontier to
  commodity to meaningless; re-derive at each milestone.
- [run-opening.md](run-opening.md) — the deterministic early chain, as a route rather than a script.
- [run-capital.md](run-capital.md) — spread Time Advancements, pick the challenge posture, and prefer
  moves that also bank cross-run capital.
- [rituals.md](rituals.md) — enter at the highest winnable level, quit only stalls, and press a
  multi-target spell rather than waiting on the auto-attack.
